## Subscription cancellation

A customer may cancel a subscription at any time. Cancellation prevents the next renewal; it does not immediately terminate access. Access continues through the end of the current paid billing period. Already billed subscription fees are non-refundable except in the duplicate-charge exception.

<!-- contextops:section=duplicate-charge -->
## Duplicate subscription charges

A verified duplicate subscription charge can be refunded if reported within 60 calendar days of that charge. The cancellation non-refundable rule does not prevent this exception. A subscription renewal is not a duplicate merely because the customer forgot to cancel.
